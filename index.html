eridus-data

eridus-data is a private tool for a single owner. It reads post statistics from the owner's own accounts. It isn't a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

Terms of Service

This tool is provided for its owner's personal use only. It's not offered to anyone else, and no other person is given access. It only reads statistics and doesn't post, comment or message on any account.

Privacy Policy

This tool only accesses the owner's own accounts, with the owner's permission. It doesn't collect, store or share information about any other person, and it doesn't sell data or show ads. Post statistics are kept privately by the owner, who can stop the tool's access at any time by removing it from their account settings.
